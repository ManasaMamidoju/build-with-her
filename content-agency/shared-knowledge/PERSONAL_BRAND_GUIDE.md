# Personal Brand Guide — Manasa / DigiMAIDS

## Core Values
- **Empowerment through automation** — help women reclaim their time
- **Accessible AI** — make technology approachable, not intimidating
- **Real over perfect** — authentic stories beat polished marketing
- **Women-first** — built by women, for women business owners
- **Results-driven** — outcomes and time savings, not features

## Brand Voice & Tone
- **Direct** — say what you mean, no fluff or corporate-speak
- **Smart & warm** — knowledgeable but never condescending
- **Relatable** — Manasa shares the real journey, not just the wins
- **Confident** — doesn't beg for attention or over-justify decisions
- **Conversational** — write like you're talking to a friend, not an audience

## Audience
- Women business owners (service-based, e-commerce, SMAs, content creators)
- Usually 28-45, established enough to know they need help
- Tech-curious but not tech-savvy — they want solutions, not explanations
- Time-starved — drowning in admin, want to focus on growth
- Values: community, authenticity, efficiency, financial freedom

## Banned Words & Phrases
Do NOT use these — they dilute the brand:
- "game-changer"
- "boss babe"
- "hustle harder" / "grind"
- "crushing it"
- "empower your journey"
- "level up your business"
- "synergy"
- "disruptive"
- "passionate about helping" (show it, don't say it)
- Excessive exclamation marks!!!
- "Just" (as in "just reach out") — weakens authority
