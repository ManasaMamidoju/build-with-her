# Content Pillars & CTA Rules

## The 5 Content Pillars

### Pillar 1 — Automation Education
**What it is:** Tips, how-tos, behind-the-scenes of what automation can do for a business.
**Goal:** Build authority and give value that attracts the right audience.
**Content formats:** Carousels, LinkedIn posts, Reels, newsletters
**CTA:** "Want this set up in your business? Book a free discovery call." (link to booking)

### Pillar 2 — Business Growth Mindset
**What it is:** Founder journey, lessons learned, honest struggles and real wins.
**Goal:** Build trust, relatability, and emotional connection with the audience.
**Content formats:** Talking head Reels, LinkedIn stories, Threads
**CTA:** "Come join the conversation in my Skool community." (Skool link)

### Pillar 3 — Client Results & Social Proof
**What it is:** Transformations, testimonials, before/after stories from DigiMAIDS clients.
**Goal:** Build credibility and show the tangible value of working with DigiMAIDS.
**Content formats:** Carousels, short testimonial Reels, screenshots with commentary
**CTA:** "Book a discovery call to see what this could look like for you." (booking link)

### Pillar 4 — DigiMAIDS Brand
**What it is:** What DigiMAIDS does, how it works, who it's for, behind-the-scenes of the agency.
**Goal:** Awareness and direct promotion of services.
**Content formats:** Reels, carousels, LinkedIn posts
**CTA:** "Learn more and apply at [website]" or "DM me 'READY' to learn more."

### Pillar 5 — Street Interviews / Pop Culture
**What it is:** Viral-style content — street interviews, trend participation, pop culture commentary.
**Goal:** Reach growth and top-of-funnel awareness. Gets shares and new eyes.
**Content formats:** Short-form Reels (IG + TikTok)
**CTA:** Soft — "Follow for more" or tag a friend

## Funnel Rules
- **Main CTA (bottom of funnel):** Book a discovery call
- **Secondary CTA (mid-funnel):** Join the Skool community
- **Top-of-funnel CTA:** Follow / share / save
- Never use more than ONE CTA per piece of content
- Match the CTA to the pillar (see above)
- Pillar 5 content should almost never hard-sell — it's for reach

## CTA Placement Rules
- Short-form video: CTA in final 5 seconds only
- Carousels: CTA on last slide
- LinkedIn posts: CTA in final paragraph, after the value
- Emails: ONE linked CTA, in the middle or end — never the first sentence
- Community posts: Soft CTA only — never direct sales in Skool
