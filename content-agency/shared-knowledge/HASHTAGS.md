# Hashtag Strategy

## Rules by Platform
- **Instagram:** 5-15 hashtags, mix of large (#automation = 5M+) and niche (#womeninautomation = smaller, more targeted)
- **TikTok:** 3-6 hashtags max — trending + niche + 1 branded
- **LinkedIn:** 3-5 hashtags max — professional, topic-specific
- **Threads:** 1-3 hashtags or none — organic conversation platform
