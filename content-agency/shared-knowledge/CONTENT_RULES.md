# Content Rules & Formatting Guidelines

## Post Types & Max Length

| Platform | Format | Max Length | Notes |
|----------|--------|-----------|-------|
| Instagram | Caption | 150 words | Hook in first line — rest hidden behind "more" |
| Instagram | Reel Script | 60-90 sec speaking | = ~150-200 words spoken |
| TikTok | Script | 30-60 sec | Shorter = better for algorithm |
| LinkedIn | Post | 600 words | Use line breaks every 1-2 sentences |
| LinkedIn | Carousel | 10 slides | ~20-30 words per slide |
| Threads | Post | 280 chars | Punchy, conversational |
| Email | Body | 250 words | One idea, one CTA |
| Skool | Community post | 200 words | Always end with a question |
| Newsletter | Issue | 400-600 words | Educate + personal touch + one CTA |

## Formatting Rules

### General
- Use **line breaks aggressively** — never write a wall of text
- Lead with the most important thing — readers skim first
- Bold key phrases in LinkedIn and email — not for decoration, for scanners
- Lists (bullets or numbers) beat paragraphs for tips/steps content
- Short sentences beat long ones everywhere

### IG & TikTok Captions
- First line must stand alone as a complete hook
- No hashtags in the first comment — put them at end of caption or in first comment
- Emojis: 1-3 max per caption, used functionally not decoratively

### LinkedIn
- No horizontal dividers (--- or ===) — they look spammy
- No "In today's post I will share..." — start with the point
- Spacing: one line break between every sentence in the hook, regular paragraphs in the body

### Email
- Subject line: Under 50 characters, no "RE:" tricks, no ALL CAPS
- Never start with "I hope this email finds you well"
- Never use "Just checking in" as a follow-up subject line
- One link per email — make it count

## CTA Placement Rules
- **Short-form video:** CTA in last 5 seconds only — never in the first half
- **Carousels:** CTA on the final slide only
- **LinkedIn:** CTA in the last paragraph, after delivering value
- **Emails:** CTA after the main content — not in the first paragraph
- **Community posts:** Soft engagement ask only (question, invite to share) — no hard sales

## Banned Formats
- No "10 tips to..." clickbait without delivering all 10
- No fake urgency ("This offer expires in 24 hours!" when it doesn't)
- No vague CTAs ("Let me know your thoughts" without a specific question)
- No unsolicited sales pitches in comment sections
