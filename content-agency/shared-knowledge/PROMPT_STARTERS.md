# Prompt Starters — Agent Activation Prompts

## How to Use
Copy any of these prompts and paste them directly into the relevant GPT agent to get a specific output.

---

## Agent 01 — Brand Voice
- "Review this post and give it a brand score. Rewrite it in Manasa's voice: [paste post]"
- "Does this email sound like me? Flag anything off-brand and fix it: [paste email]"
- "I wrote this LinkedIn caption. Is it too corporate? Make it more me: [paste text]"

## Agent 02 — Content Strategy
- "Build me a content calendar for the week of [date]. I have a discovery call push happening."
- "I need 30 days of content ideas across all 5 pillars. Give me a batched content plan."
- "Which pillar am I neglecting? Here are my last 10 posts: [list topics]"

## Agent 03 — Script Writer
- "Write an IG Reel script about [topic]. Target: women business owners. CTA: book a discovery call."
- "Script a 45-second talking head video on why automation isn't just for big companies."
- "Write an AI clone script about [topic] — clean sentences, no filler words."
- "I'm doing a street interview about [question]. Write my intro hook and outro."

## Agent 04 — Long-Form Writer
- "Write a LinkedIn post about [topic]. Use my founder story angle. Pillar: Business Growth Mindset."
- "Write this week's newsletter. Topic: [topic]. Include 1 automation tip + soft discovery call CTA."
- "Turn this Reel script into a LinkedIn thought leadership post: [paste script]"

## Agent 05 — Visual Content
- "Create a 10-slide carousel outline about [topic]. Platform: Instagram. Pillar: Automation Education."
- "Write a Canva brief for a quote card. Quote: [quote]. Brand colors, clean and bold."
- "Plan the visual direction for next week's content batch. Topics: [list them]."

## Agent 06 — Email & Funnel
- "Write a 5-email welcome sequence for new Skool members."
- "Write a 3-email nurture sequence for warm leads who haven't booked yet."
- "Write a sales email for the Deep Clean package. Angle: time savings."
- "Write a re-engagement email for subscribers who haven't opened in 60 days."

## Agent 07 — Community & Engagement
- "Write 5 Skool community posts for this week — include a resource drop and a discussion starter."
- "Write a cold DM script for new followers who engage with my automation content."
- "Write post-event follow-up DMs for [event name] attendees."
- "Give me 10 comment response templates for people asking 'how do I start with automation?'"

## Agent 08 — Analytics & Strategy
- "Here are my KPIs for this week: [paste numbers]. Give me my weekly performance report."
- "My engagement dropped 30% this week. Here's what I posted: [list]. What happened?"
- "Which content format is performing best this month? Here's my data: [paste data]"
- "Do a monthly content audit. Here are my top and bottom 5 posts: [list them]"
