# Batch Content Workflow

Use this when you want to create 2-4 weeks of content in one focused session (typically 3-4 hours).

---

## When to Batch
- Before a vacation or busy period
- After a launch when you need to refill the pipeline
- Monthly content planning sessions

---

## Step 1 — Content Audit (15 min)
**Agent:** 08-Analytics & Strategy

Review what performed best last month.
Prompt: "Here are my top 10 posts from last month: [list]. What themes and formats should I double down on?"

---

## Step 2 — Topic Bank (20 min)
**Agent:** 02-Content Strategy

Generate 30+ topic ideas across all 5 pillars.
Prompt: "Give me 30 content ideas for [month]. Spread across all 5 pillars. Include format recommendation for each."

Choose 20-25 to execute. Assign to weeks.

---

## Step 3 — Script Batch (90 min)
**Agent:** 03-Script Writer

Write all Reel and talking head scripts in one session.
For each topic: "Write a 45-second Reel script about [topic]. CTA: [CTA]."

Save all to `content-output/reels/` with date-coded filenames.

---

## Step 4 — Long-Form Batch (60 min)
**Agent:** 04-Long-Form Writer

Write all LinkedIn posts and newsletters in sequence.
For each: "Write a LinkedIn post about [topic]. Pillar: [pillar]."

Save all to `content-output/linkedin/` or schedule directly.

---

## Step 5 — Carousel Batch (45 min)
**Agent:** 05-Visual Content

Create all carousel outlines and Canva briefs.
For each: "Create a [N]-slide carousel outline about [topic]. Platform: IG."

Send outlines to designer or use with Canva AI.
Save to `content-output/carousels/`.

---

## Step 6 — Email Batch (30 min)
**Agent:** 06-Email & Funnel

Write 2-4 weeks of nurture emails or the current sequence.
Prompt: "Write emails 3-6 of my nurture sequence. Context: [where subscribers are in the funnel]."

Save to `content-output/emails/`.

---

## Step 7 — Brand Review Pass (30 min)
**Agent:** 01-Brand Voice

Spot-check 20% of the batch — especially any AI-generated content you're unsure about.
Prompt: "Review this [format] for brand voice. Score it and fix anything off: [paste]."

---

## Step 8 — Schedule Everything (30 min)
Load all approved content into RecurPost / Buffer / Later.
Map out the full calendar in Notion / ClickUp.
Flag any pieces that still need video recording or design work.

---

## Batch Session Total Time: ~5-6 hours
Best done in two 3-hour blocks, or one full day session.
