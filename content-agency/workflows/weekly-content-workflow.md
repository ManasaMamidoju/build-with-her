# Weekly Content Workflow

Run this every Sunday to plan and execute the coming week's content.

---

## Step 1 — Strategy Session (Sunday, 30 min)
**Agent:** 02-Content Strategy

Prompt: "Build me a content calendar for the week of [date]. Any active campaigns or launches: [yes/no + details]."

Output: Filled content calendar grid (date, platform, format, pillar, topic, CTA).
Save to: Notion / ClickUp content calendar

---

## Step 2 — Script Batch (Sunday or Monday, 60 min)
**Agent:** 03-Script Writer

For each Reel/TikTok/talking head in the calendar:
Prompt: "Write a [format] script about [topic]. Platform: [IG/TikTok]. CTA: [CTA from calendar]."

Output: 2-3 script variations per video
Save to: `content-output/reels/` folder, named `YYYY-MM-DD_topic.md`

---

## Step 3 — Long-Form Batch (Monday, 45 min)
**Agent:** 04-Long-Form Writer

For each LinkedIn post and newsletter in the calendar:
Prompt: "Write a LinkedIn post about [topic]. Pillar: [pillar]. Voice: Manasa."

Output: Final post copy + hashtags
Save to: `content-output/linkedin/` or email drafts

---

## Step 4 — Visual Briefs (Monday, 30 min)
**Agent:** 05-Visual Content

For each carousel or graphic in the calendar:
Prompt: "Create a carousel outline for [topic]. Platform: IG. [number] slides."

Output: Slide-by-slide outline + Canva brief
Save to: `content-output/carousels/` and send to designer / Canva AI

---

## Step 5 — Brand Review (Monday or Tuesday, 20 min)
**Agent:** 01-Brand Voice

Paste each piece of content for a quick brand check:
Prompt: "Review this post and flag anything off-brand: [paste content]"

Only needed for content that wasn't written by the agents. If agents wrote it, spot-check 1-2 per week.

---

## Step 6 — Schedule Content (Tuesday, 20 min)
Load approved content into your scheduling tool (RecurPost / Buffer / Later).
- IG + TikTok Reels: schedule for peak times (check analytics for your audience)
- LinkedIn: Tuesday-Thursday, 8-10am or 12-1pm
- Emails: Tuesday or Thursday morning

---

## Step 7 — Community Posts (Wednesday, 15 min)
**Agent:** 07-Community & Engagement

Prompt: "Write 3 Skool posts for this week: 1 resource drop, 1 discussion starter, 1 check-in."

Post manually in Skool. Do not automate community posts — authenticity matters here.

---

## Step 8 — DM & Engagement Round (Friday, 30 min)
**Agent:** 07-Community & Engagement

- Reply to all week's comments using engagement templates
- Send follow-up DMs to warm leads who engaged
- DM new followers who look like ideal clients

Prompt: "Write follow-up DMs for [X] people who commented on my [topic] post."

---

## Step 9 — Weekly KPI Log (Sunday, 15 min)
**Agent:** 08-Analytics & Strategy

Log your weekly numbers in Airtable / Notion:
- Reach, engagement rate, top post
- Calls booked, new Skool members
- Email open rate

Prompt: "Here are my KPIs for this week: [paste numbers]. Give me my weekly report and 3 action items."

---
